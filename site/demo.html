Are these real?

The samples are short, inert snippets carrying the same indicators real threats carry. None of them can do anything to your machine.

Is the engine real?

Yes. This is a port of the actual detection layers, not a scripted demo. The desktop product adds archive scanning, native YARA, PE parsing and the full signature feed.

Why false positives matter. Catching every threat is easy if you block everything. The hard part is leaving safe files alone. Try launcher_log.txt or the Windows forwarder DLL: both look alarming by the numbers and neither raises an alarm. Where the engine cannot tell two things apart it says suspicious and leaves the file where it is — and anything it does move, it can move back.
