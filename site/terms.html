Who is responsible

This website

  • Hosting. The site is hosted by Vercel Inc. (USA). Vercel processes connection data (IP address, time, requested file, browser) to deliver the pages and protect them against abuse. Legal basis: legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Vercel is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • No cookies, no tracking. No analytics, no ad networks, no third-party embeds. Scripts, styles and images all come from this site.

  • Downloads and source. The download buttons and the source links go to GitHub (GitHub Inc., USA), which serves the files and receives your IP address like any server. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

The software

  • No telemetry, no account, no cloud lookups. Files, hashes and scan results are checked on your computer and never sent anywhere. Sebi receives none of it.
  • What stays on your computer. Settings, scan history, detections (file paths and hashes), an event log and quarantined files live in one folder: %PROGRAMDATA%\Guardiantus on Windows, ~/Library/Application Support/Guardiantus on macOS, ~/.local/share/guardiantus on Linux. Quarantine entries older than 90 days (adjustable) are removed when the next file is quarantined. Deleting the folder removes everything.
  • The dashboard runs on your own computer (127.0.0.1) and cannot be reached from other devices.
  • Program updates. "Watch for outdated programs" is on by default: once a day, Guardiantus asks the package managers on your computer which programs are out of date (winget and Chocolatey on Windows, Homebrew on macOS, apt, dnf, pacman, Flatpak and Snap on Linux, and pip). Several of them contact their own package sources to answer, under their own privacy terms; Guardiantus adds nothing of its own. Installing stays up to you. Turn it off in Settings → Updates.
  • Signature feed. Only if you enter a feed address in Settings does Guardiantus download signature sets from it; that server sees your IP address. By default the built-in set is used and nothing is downloaded.
